Crit is 0 on a default Hercules server: enable_critical: 17 only gives crits to players and mercenaries. VIT doesn't raise a homunculus's Max HP and INT doesn't raise its Max SP; HP and SP are separate rolls each level.

How the math works

Growth. Each level, every stat rolls a random number between the homunculus's minimum and maximum growth (in tenths of a point) and the server throws away the fraction (homunculus_levelup). HP and SP roll whole numbers. Evolution adds a one-time bonus to every stat; the evolved form keeps the same growth table. The odds for this homunculus:

Odds. All chances are exact distributions (sums of those per-level rolls), not approximations.

Keep or restart. The advice minimises the total homunculus EXP you expect to spend before you own a level 99 homunculus that meets your target, counting the restart cost you chose. At each level it compares two options: keep going, and restart later if it falls behind, or start over now. The keep line is the lowest stat at which keeping is cheaper. Early levels cost almost no EXP, so the keep line starts out strict and relaxes as more EXP is at stake.

Build score. Your priority order weights the six stats: the #1 stat counts 6×, #2 counts 5×, and so on down to 1× for #6. The score's percentile is exact too.
